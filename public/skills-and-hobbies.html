My Skills and Hobbies

English Language

The one I find most useful is my knowledge of the English language.

I learned this skill mostly in a passive way: browsing the internet from a young age,
having odd interests such as niche shows and videogames (at least, by Italy's standards)
and generally having my big sister as my guide on the topic.

During high school, I was able to acquire the Certificate in Advanced English (C1)
through my teacher's recommendation, and though it's unrenewed, I retain the skills.

I'm more advanced in reading and writing and listening than in speaking, because
because I've had little chance to practice, but it's only an accent shortcoming.

Writing and Reading

I've had a keen interest in writing for as far as I can remember.

My fascination, in this case too due to my big sister, is caused by the possibility of
easily (or not so easily) creating an entire world from my mind and putting it on paper,
expressing what cannot be said by mere words through character arcs and conflict
making even one person feel seen through relatability and appeal.

In this sense, I've always been close to hobbies like Dungeons and Dragons and art.

Music

Music is another mean through which I express the unsaid.

I got close to this medium when I picked up my first guitar at age 10... I remember
I spent the whole night reading my music class book. The whole of it. I kept scrolling
through the pages, wondering if I'd be able to play all the different songs, even the ones
I never heard of... Well, I wasn't. Not the day after, nor the day after that.
But eventually, I became skilled enough to dedicate myself to the natural evolution of
every rookie musician: DAW's.

Although it's an art form I haven't been giving the right amount of attention to, I'm
still able to know my way around FL Studio and Audacity.

And I'll never abandon music completely, because my final goal would be...

Videogames

All my interests lead here, inevitably.

What I consider to be the ultimate expression of art humankind can achieve, videogames
have always been the background of my life.

From the classics that shaped my life such as Final Fantasy VII on PSX to the games which
got me closer to my lifelong friends like Super Smash Bros. on 3ds, the impact of this medium
on my life cannot be understated or denied.

I mean, I have a blog where I share my thoughts on the writing of videogames and such on Tumblr!

My ultimate life goal is clear as day: I want to create a videogame I can call my own.

May it be unsucessful, may one person play it, but as long as it will have touched their heart,
then I can say I will be fulfilled.
